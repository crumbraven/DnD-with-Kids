# Chapter 3: The Wobbly Bridge

> In the morning you reach the Rushing River. There's only one way across: a
> rickety rope bridge that wobbles even when nobody's on it. And underneath,
> snoring so loudly the ropes shake, is a TROLL.

## Scene 1: Bort the Bridge Troll
**Bort** is huge, green and grumpy because nobody ever says hello; they just
sneak across. He demands a toll: "ONE THING THAT MAKES BORT HAPPY!"
- Kids can tell a joke, sing, give a gift, play a game, or try to sneak by
  (Sneaking: every hero rolls d20 (12+); any natural 1 wakes Bort with a ROAR, harmless but
  startling).
- Fighting Bort is hard (Bridge Troll stats in `rules/MONSTERS.md`: 30 hearts, regrows, and a snore-sneeze that knocks
  everyone back). Hint early that he seems lonely.
- If befriended, Bort carries them across on his shoulders and gives them a
  **Troll Toenail** ("Lucky! Very lucky!"). Gross, but it gives +1 to one roll.

## Scene 2: Halfway Across
The bridge snaps a rope! Each hero describes how they hold on or help someone.
Teamwork bonuses encouraged. Quack the duck falls in and must be rescued.

## Scene 3: The River Sisters
Three water spirits rise from the river. They saw the starlight pass. They'll
tell the heroes about Nimbus if the heroes solve a riddle:
- "The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?" (*Footsteps*)
- Easier version: "I'm full of holes but I still hold water. What am I?" (*A sponge*)
If stuck, Clever roll (12+) for a hint.

Their secret: "The light was taken by a little moon dragon. She is not wicked.
She is *afraid*." (This is a key moment. Let the kids talk about what that means.)

## Ending
The foot of Hush Mountain. A path of glowing stones spirals upward, and at the
very top, a soft silver glow pulses like a heartbeat.
