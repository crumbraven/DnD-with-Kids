# Chapter 5: The Moon Dragon

> Inside the cave, every wall is covered in captured starlight, so bright it's
> like standing inside a star. And in the middle, curled in a ball, is a dragon
> the size of a pony, with silver scales and big, wet eyes. "Please," she
> whispers, "don't make it dark."

## Scene 1: Meeting Nimbus
Nimbus explains (in a small, shy voice) that her mama flew away to find a new
sky, and the nights are so dark and long, and she's scared. She didn't know the
village needed the light.

Let the kids lead this conversation. There's no roll needed for kindness here,
just ideas. Good solutions include:
- Giving her Ma Fennick's empty Glow Lantern to fill with *one* bit of
  starlight, a night-light of her own.
- Teaching her that the stars come back every night; the dark doesn't last.
- Promising to visit, or inviting her to live near Puddlebrook.
- Sharing their own fears (very powerful; give a ⭐).
- Whatever creative idea the kids invent.

## Scene 2: Returning the Light
Nimbus sings a *happy* song this time, and the starlight streams down the
mountain like a silver river. The heroes can ride on her back down to the
village (a big, exciting flying scene: describe the wind, the stars, the
village lights flickering on one by one below).

## Scene 3: The Festival of Lights
Puddlebrook throws a festival. Mayor Wobblebottom gives a speech (and finds his
spectacles). Every hero gets honey buns and a medal. Ask each kid: *"What's your
hero's favorite part of the festival?"* Bort, the River Sisters, Professor Hoot
and maybe even a reformed Grizzlewick show up.

## The Very End (read aloud)

> And from that night on, whenever the children of Puddlebrook were scared of
> the dark, they'd look up at the mountain and see a little silver glow, a
> moon dragon's night-light, and they'd remember the brave, clever, kind heroes
> who brought the light home.
>
> **THE END... for now.**

## What Next?
Ask the kids what adventure they want next. Ideas: Nimbus gets a message from
her mother in the stars; Grizzlewick opens a (honest?) shop that gets robbed;
the ducks declare war on the geese. Write the next campaign together with Claude.
