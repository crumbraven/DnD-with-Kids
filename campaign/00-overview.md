# The Lost Lanterns of Puddlebrook

*A five-chapter adventure. Each chapter is about one 45 to 60 minute session.*

## The Big Secret (Game Master only; don't tell the kids!)

Puddlebrook is a cozy village of hobbit-sized houses, frog ferrymen and very
nosy ducks. Every night its **Glow Lanterns** light the streets. They're magic
lanterns filled with captured starlight from the Moonwell up on Hush Mountain.

Three nights ago every lantern went dark, and the starlight vanished.

**Who did it?** Nobody evil. **Nimbus**, a young moon dragon, lives on top of
Hush Mountain. Her mother flew away to the far stars to "find a new sky", and
Nimbus is lonely and afraid of the dark. She's been calling the starlight
to her with a sad song so her cave is never dark. She doesn't know she's
hurting anyone.

Along the way, **Grizzlewick**, a greedy goblin peddler, has been *selling*
fake "starlight" (jars of fireflies and glitter) to scared villagers and wants
the dark to last forever. He's the real nuisance and a fun, silly villain.

**The happy ending:** the heroes reach Nimbus and help her not be afraid of
the dark. They can give her a lantern of her own, teach her the stars come
back every night, or promise to visit. She returns the light, and Puddlebrook
throws a festival in their honor. (Fighting Nimbus should never be the
answer. If kids try, she just cries a big glowing tear that puts out their
torch, and they realize she's sad, not mean.)

## Chapters

1. `01-dark-village.md`: Puddlebrook goes dark; find the clue.
2. `02-whisperwood.md`: Through the Whisperwood; Grizzlewick appears.
3. `03-wobbly-bridge.md`: The troll bridge and the river spirits.
4. `04-hush-mountain.md`: The climb and Grizzlewick's last trick.
5. `05-moon-dragon.md`: Meeting Nimbus; the Festival of Lights.

Feel free to stretch any chapter over two sessions, skip scenes, or invent new
ones when the kids wander off the path. **The kids' ideas beat this script.**

## Recurring Friends

- **Mayor Wobblebottom**: round, flustered, always losing his spectacles
  (they're on his head).
- **Old Ma Fennick**: the lantern-keeper, a tiny grandma who knows everything
  and gives out cookies. She can heal 1 heart with a cookie once per chapter.
- **Quack**: a duck who decides to follow the heroes. Extremely brave,
  extremely unhelpful. Kids will love him; use him for comic relief.
