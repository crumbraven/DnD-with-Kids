# Quest 3: The Heart of the Arc

*Location: the Castle Arc and the labyrinth beneath it. Two sessions.*

## Background (Game Master only)

The Castle Arc was built by the Son. Above ground and a few levels below, it's
a magnificent castle. Below that is a **labyrinth** that protects the **Ark of
the Covenant**. The Empress will allow worthy heroes to bring the Ark out to
destroy the portal. It must be **carried on its poles by four bearers** and
**never touched**: anyone who touches the Ark dies instantly. (The heroes
are four. That's not an accident.)

**Uncle Lucius** can't enter the holy castle, but the deepest labyrinth
connects to the catacombs, where holiness is weaker. He'll meet them down
there, guide them, and try to get them killed. At the Ark, he reveals himself.

## Cast

- **Empress Mondenkind** ("the Moonchild"): good, wise, warm, a little
  fierce. Head of the Church of the Son.
- **Sir Gideon**: the Empress's knight-captain, gruff and loyal. Trains boys.
- **Sister Adah**: young priestess. Trains girls; goes with the team if no
  hero can perform miracles.
- **Uncle Lucius**: see above. **This is his betrayal quest.**

## Part 1: Audience with the Empress (read aloud)

> The throne room is so tall that birds fly near the ceiling. Sunlight pours
> through windows of colored glass, painting the floor like a rainbow. At the
> end of a long carpet sits a woman in white and gold, with a silver crown
> shaped like a crescent moon. She stands up... and walks down to meet *you*. "I have heard
> what you did in the catacombs," says Empress Mondenkind. "Tell me why you've come."

- Let the kids explain. The 13-year-old can do the talking, but the Empress
  should ask each kid one question ("And what is your cat's name, little one?").
- She grants permission, gives the team **100 gold**, the **Seal of the
  Empress** (a medallion; Arc guardians won't attack whoever carries it), and
  offers **training** before they go down (`rules/SHOP.md`).
- **A good moment for a Lucky Coin clue:** if a hero carries Lucius's coin,
  it gets warm when they enter the throne room, and the Empress frowns. "Where
  did you get that?" If they bless it at the castle chapel, it becomes a holy
  medallion (+1 Armor).

## Room 1: The Gate of Trials (guardians)

> Far below the castle, past the wine cellars and the old dungeons, is a
> door of white stone, taller than a house. Beside it stand two statues of
> knights. As you step closer, their stone eyes begin to glow.

- **2 Arc Sentinels** (use Mossy Golem stats: Armor 16 · Hearts 22 · +4 ·
  d10, attack every other round). They are **not evil**.
- **Showing the Seal of the Empress** makes them kneel and step aside. If the
  heroes forgot the Seal, they must fight, or answer the Sentinels' question:
  each hero must say **one true thing** about themselves (truth opens the gate).

## Room 2: The Shifting Halls (the labyrinth)

> The walls here are painted with pictures: a baby in a manger, a carpenter,
> a man walking on water. And as you watch, the walls *move*, grinding and
> sliding, making new halls and closing old ones.

Three junctions. At each one, describe three paths. **The right path always
follows the next picture in the story of the Son** (painted on the walls):
1. **Junction 1:** a painting of the Son as a child. Paths show a carpenter's
   workshop (✔), a dragon, and a crown.
2. **Junction 2:** paths show a feast with fish and bread (✔), a sword, and a
   tower.
3. **Junction 3:** paths show a hill with three crosses (✔ if they reason
   it through, or a hero rolls 12+ to recall), a throne, and a treasure chest.

**Let the 4-year-old pick at least one path.** Wrong paths aren't the end:
each one leads to an encounter, then back:
- a **Ghoul** that has crept up from the catacombs,
- **3 Skeleton Warriors** and **1 Skeleton Archer**,
- a **treasure room**: **4d6 gold** and a **Blessed Mace**.

## Room 3: The Friendly Shortcut (setback)

> A familiar voice echoes down the hall. "Yoo-hoo! Over here, my dears!" It's
> Uncle Lucius, leaning on his walking stick, his bag of sweets jingling. "I
> came in the back way, through the catacombs. I know a shortcut!"

- He's charming. He gives out sweets. **The cats hiss and puff up.** If
  anyone has holy water, he keeps his distance. His shadow on the wall has
  **horns** (roll 12+ to notice, or automatic for the 13-year-old if they've
  been suspicious).
- His shortcut leads into a collapsed hall where the catacombs break
  through: a **Zombie Horde** (Armor 8 · Hearts 40). Lucius says, "Oh dear!
  I'll go get help!" and vanishes.
- If the heroes refuse the shortcut, he just smiles, "Suit yourselves!", and
  the Horde finds them anyway, smaller (30 hearts).

### ⏸ Good stopping point
> Past the Horde, the hall ends in a pair of golden doors, and light,
> real warm golden light, is shining through the cracks. It feels like a hug.
> You've found the Heart of the Arc.

## Room 4: The Heart of the Arc (the betrayal)

> Inside is a small, plain room, and in the middle sits a golden chest with
> two golden angels on the lid, their wings touching. Long golden poles run
> through rings on its sides. The air hums. You've never felt so safe in your
> whole life. This is the **Ark of the Covenant**.

- **Make the Ark rule clear:** a voice, or Sister Adah, or a carved warning,
  says it must be **carried by its poles** and **never touched**.
- **Lucius arrives** at the door. He *cannot* step inside the golden light.
  "Wonderful! Now, dear, just lift the lid and take the power inside. Go on,
  touch it, it won't hurt." **(It would kill them.)** Let them decide. If a
  kid reaches for it, have the cat leap onto their hand to stop them.
- When they refuse (or catch him lying), **Lucius reveals himself**:

> Uncle Lucius laughs, and the laugh gets deeper and deeper. His walking
> stick turns into a black pitchfork. His kind face cracks like an eggshell,
> and underneath it is something with red eyes and curling horns. "You
> sweet, silly children. Who do you think gave the witches that ritual?"

- **The Devil fights** (revealed stats in `rules/MONSTERS.md`) with **3 Imps**.
  He stays **outside** the Ark's light; any hero standing in the light gets
  **+2 Armor**.
- **He cannot be destroyed while the portal is open.** After **3 rounds**, or
  at 0 hearts, he melts into shadow: "You'll never get that box to
  Xiphex. I'll be waiting!"

## Room 5: Bearers of the Ark (reward)

- The four heroes lift the Ark by its poles. **A big moment.** Read it slowly.
- While the Ark is carried, **ordinary undead can't come within 10 steps of
  it**. Demons and Shiva *can*.
- Back in the throne room, the Empress names them **Bearers of the Ark**.
  Every hero gets **50 gold**, and the team **levels up** (if they haven't
  already).

## Conclusion

> "The army marches for Xiphex at dawn," says the Empress. "They will
> fight the horde at the walls. But the Ark must reach the portal itself...
> and only you can get it there." In its basket, Cinder the grey cat opens its
> orange eyes and purrs.
