# Campaign: The Portal of Xiphex

*Four quests, each about two short sessions (30–60 minutes each).*

## The Story in One Breath (Game Master only)

The dead are rising because a coven of witches in **Xiphex** accidentally
opened a **portal to Hell**, guarded by the demon **Shiva**. The ritual was
given to them by **Uncle Lucius**, a kindly old traveler who is really **the
Devil**. To end the apocalypse, the heroes must:

1. **The Bones Beneath** (`quest-1-catacombs.md`): clear the catacombs under
   their home town, rescue a runaway young witch, and learn the truth.
2. **Catch a Cat, Catch a Sky** (`quest-2-floating-island.md`): catch a
   blue-and-green cat to reach the Floating Island and find the coven's Elder,
   who knows how the portal can be destroyed.
3. **The Heart of the Arc** (`quest-3-castle-arc.md`): win the Empress's
   blessing, brave the labyrinth beneath the Castle Arc to reach the **Ark of
   the Covenant**, and survive Uncle Lucius's betrayal.
4. **The Gates of Xiphex** (`quest-4-xiphex.md`): carry the Ark into
   the witches' town, defeat Shiva, and destroy the portal.

## Uncle Lucius Through the Campaign

| Quest | What Lucius does | Clues for sharp eyes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gives each hero a gift: a Healing Potion and a **Lucky Coin** | Won't step into the temple ("my old knees, dear") |
| 2 | Gives the heroes **cat treats** to catch the cat. Really helpful! | The cat hisses at him; holy water makes him sneeze |
| 3 | Guides them in the labyrinth, then **betrays them** at the Ark | His shortcut leads into a trap; his shadow has horns |
| 4 | Lies, tempts, and fights beside Shiva | — |

**The Lucky Coin:** once per day, +1 to any roll. It really works. It's also
how Lucius tracks them. If a hero gets it blessed at a temple (free) it turns
into a holy medallion (+1 Armor) and he can't track them anymore. Don't explain
any of this unless they figure it out.

## Leveling Pace

Stars (⭐) drive leveling. Aim for roughly: level 2 during Quest 1, level 3 by
the end of Quest 2, level 4 by the end of Quest 3, and level 5 for the finale.
If they're behind, hand out more stars.

## Running Short Sessions

Every quest file has a **⏸ Good stopping point** marked. If the session is
running long, any room boundary is a fine place to stop, too. End on a
cliffhanger whenever you can.
