# Quest 2: Catch a Cat, Catch a Sky

*Location: the home town, then the Floating Island. Two sessions.*

## Background (Game Master only)

**Grandmother Morwen**, Elder of the Hollowmere coven, refused the ritual and
fled to the **Floating Island** with her books. She knows the portal can only
be destroyed by the holiest power in the world: **the Ark of the Covenant**,
beneath the Castle Arc. Lucius knows she's there and has sent **Hellhounds**
to silence her. The heroes must get there first.

The island itself is a peaceful place of floating rocks, wind chimes and
hundreds of blue-and-green cats. The apocalypse has reached it too: a
**skeleton sky-pirate ship** has crashed into it.

## Cast

- **The Sky Cat**: blue legs and belly, green back. The kids name it! Clever,
  fast and a bit smug.
- **Uncle Lucius**: shows up with **cat treats**. ("Every cat in the world
  loves these, dear!") They work (+2 on catching rolls). Cats hiss at him.
- **Captain Clatterjaw**: skeleton sky-pirate captain, wears a tricorn hat
  over his skull. Not evil exactly, just undead and greedy.
- **Grandmother Morwen**: ancient, sharp-tongued, kind underneath. Has a
  crow named Pickle.

## Hook (read aloud)

> It's a sunny morning, and there it is: on the roof of the bakery, licking
> its paw. A cat with **bright blue legs and belly** and a **green back**,
> like a little piece of sky sitting on top of a hill. A cat from the
> Floating Island! It looks right at you, flicks its tail... and leaps to the
> next roof.

## Part 1: The Great Cat Chase (in town)

A chase across the town. **The heroes need 3 successes before 3 misses.**
Each hero takes a turn describing how they try: climbing roofs, setting a
trap, calling it, offering food, cornering it in an alley.
- Rolls are **12+**. **Cat treats** give +2. A great idea gets +2 or an
  automatic success.
- **The 4-year-old's turn:** the cat should *choose* her. If she offers it
  love or food, it just walks up to her and rubs against her legs. It works.
- **Each miss** is a funny disaster: falling into the fishmonger's cart, the
  cat stealing a hat, getting chased by geese.
- Uncle Lucius can appear partway through and hand out the treats (the cat
  hisses at him).
- **When the cat is caught,** everyone touching the cat (or touching someone
  touching it) **slips through the Veils**:

> The world goes soft and shimmery, like looking through a soap bubble. Your
> tummy flips. You hear a sound like a curtain being pulled aside... and
> WIND! You're standing on a rock in the middle of the sky, with clouds
> *below* you.

## Room 1: The Wreck of the *Rattling Gull* (guardians)

> The island is beautiful: floating rocks connected by rope bridges, wind
> chimes everywhere, and cats, *hundreds* of blue-and-green cats. But right in
> the middle, a wooden ship has smashed into the hillside. Its sails are
> torn. And on its deck, skeletons in pirate hats are throwing cats into
> sacks!

- **Captain Clatterjaw** (Skeleton Warrior with 18 hearts and a d8 cutlass)
  plus **2 Skeleton Warriors** and **2 Skeleton Archers** (in the rigging).
- Freeing the cats makes the island's cats grateful: every hero gets
  **+1 on catching rolls forever** (cats remember).
- **Treasure:** the captain's chest: **4d6 gold**, a **Spyglass**, and a
  pirate hat that makes you talk like a pirate.

## Room 2: The Wind Bridges (puzzle)

> Between you and the tallest floating rock is a chain of rope bridges, and
> the wind is HOWLING. The bridges swing like jump ropes.

- The wind blows in a pattern, and the wind chimes ring just before a gust.
  Clever kids notice: **cross only when the chimes are quiet.**
- Each hero crosses: roll **10+** (or automatic if they wait for the chimes).
  A miss means they're swinging from the rope! Another hero can help (+2) or
  they take **d4** when they bonk the rock.

## Room 3: The Harpy Nest (setback)

> Halfway up the tall rock, three women with wings instead of arms and
> claws instead of feet burst out of a nest. They SHRIEK.

- **3 Storm Harpies:** Armor 12 · Hearts 10 · Attack +4 · d6 claws. They fly
  (up-close weapons only when they swoop). **Song:** once per fight, one
  hero must roll 12+ or walk toward the edge, losing their turn. A friend
  can grab them.
- **Treasure:** the nest has **3d6 gold** and a **giant egg** (it hatches
  later into... the Game Master's choice).

### ⏸ Good stopping point
> At the top of the rock is a crooked little tower. Its door has been
> ripped off. Inside, you hear growling, and an old woman shouting
> "Get out of my house, you overgrown hot-water bottles!"

## Room 4: Morwen's Tower (the boss)

> Two dogs made of smoke and fire are circling an old woman in a nightgown.
> She's swinging a frying pan at them. Their eyes glow like coals, and the
> floor smokes where they step.

- **2 Hellhounds** (`rules/MONSTERS.md`). These are **demons**: Holy Burst
  hits them for 3d6, holy damage is doubled, and **fire does nothing**.
- Morwen helps: once per round she can throw a hex (one hound has −2 to hit).
- If a Hellhound is about to die, it howls "THE MASTER WILL FIND YOU!" before
  vanishing into sulfur smoke.

## Room 5: Morwen's Secrets (reward and revelation)

Morwen makes tea (it tastes like clouds) and tells them:
- "The portal can't be closed with witchcraft. It needs the **holiest power
  in the world**: the **Ark of the Covenant**, deep beneath the **Castle
  Arc**. Only the **Empress** can allow you to go there."
- "The man who gave us the ritual... I never saw his face. But beware
  kindness that asks for nothing. It always wants something later."
- She gives them a **Hollowmere cat** in a basket: sooty grey, with
  **ember-orange eyes**, named **Cinder**. "When the time comes, Cinder will
  take you home to Hollowmere, right past the horde."
- She sends them to the Castle Arc with a **gold-and-white cat** from there,
  **Sir Whiskerton**.
- **Treasure:** Morwen gives one item from her chest to each hero. Let them
  choose from: **Elven Cloak**, **Frost Staff**, **Lucky Charm**, **Healing
  Potion ×2**, **Friendship Flute**.
