# Chapter 4: Hush Mountain

> Hush Mountain is quiet. So quiet you can hear your own heartbeat. Snow
> sparkles on the path. Above you, the silver glow pulses, and the sad song
> floats down on the wind.

## Scene 1: The Climb
A steep icy section. Let each hero say how they help the team get up:
climbing, magic, building steps, carrying Quack. Three rolls of 12+ get
everyone up. Each miss = a funny slide back down (lose d4 hearts or drop an item).

## Scene 2: The Echo Cave
A cave that echoes everything... but changes the words. Whatever the heroes
say comes back sillier ("Hello!" → "Jell-O!"). A **Snow Owl** named Professor
Hoot lives here and will guide them to the top if they make her laugh with the
echoes.

## Scene 3: Grizzlewick's Last Trick
Grizzlewick is back (unless he was befriended), riding a rickety
**Gadget-Golem** made of pots, pans and jars. He wants to catch the dragon and
keep her so it stays dark forever and he gets rich.
- Gadget-Golem stats are in `rules/MONSTERS.md` (Armor 15, 24 hearts).
- A sharp-eyed hero might notice its off switch is labeled "DO NOT PRESS".
- A friendly hero might convince Grizzlewick that being liked is better than being
  rich (he's never had a friend either).
- If Snip and Snorkel were befriended, they show up and sabotage the Golem.

Whatever happens, Grizzlewick ends up stuck in a snowdrift, sneezing.
Let the kids decide what to do with him. (Mercy should earn a ⭐.)

## Ending
At the summit, a silver cave glows. Inside, someone is crying softly.
