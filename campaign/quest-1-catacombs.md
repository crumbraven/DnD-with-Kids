# Quest 1: The Bones Beneath

*Location: the catacombs under the heroes' home town. Two sessions.*

## Background (Game Master only)

Three nights ago the old crypt beneath the town's temple **cracked open** into
the deep catacombs, and skeletons have been climbing up at night. They aren't
random. A witch who went bad, **Sister Morda**, is down there raising the dead
to hunt a runaway: **Euonie**, a 13-year-old apprentice witch from Xiphex who
fled through the catacombs after the ritual went wrong. Euonie knows the truth
about the portal.

## Cast

- **Mother Ilsa**: High Mother (High Priestess) of the town temple. Kind,
  stern, tired. Quest-giver.
- **Anamata**: the High Mother's guard. A tall, steady temple warrior in
  white-and-silver armor marked with the sunburst. She wields a huge
  **two-handed mace**, and sometimes switches to a spiked **flail** that she
  whirls over her head. Few words, fierce loyalty, secretly soft-hearted (she's the one
  who feeds Pudding the cat extra treats). A good ally and a role model for
  brave girls.
- **Pudding**: the temple's fat orange cat (born here, so orange like the
  temple's sandstone). Missing! Went down into the catacombs.
- **Uncle Lucius**: a jolly old traveler with a walking stick and a bag of
  sweets. (Secretly the Devil; see `00-campaign.md`.)
- **Old Bartholomew**: a talking skull in the catacombs. Not undead, just
  enchanted ages ago. Loves terrible jokes.
- **Euonie**: young witch, scared, brave, guilty. Ally.
- **Sister Morda**: a bad witch, now a **Necromancer** (stats in
  `rules/MONSTERS.md`, hearts 25).

## Hook (read aloud)

> The temple bell is ringing in the middle of the night. *DONG. DONG. DONG.*
> But nobody is pulling the rope. You run to the temple and find Mother Ilsa
> on the steps, holding a lantern. Beside her stands her guard, Anamata, tall
> and silent in silver armor, her great mace resting on
> her shoulder, still dusty with smashed bones. "They came up
> from the crypt again," she says. "Five of them this time. The knights are
> away fighting at the walls. And Pudding, our temple cat, chased one down
> the stairs and hasn't come back." She looks at each of you. "I need heroes
> to go down into the catacombs, find out what's raising the dead... and
> bring Pudding home."

**Reward offered:** 50 gold, free healing at the temple, and a flask of Holy
Water each.

**Anamata stays behind** to guard the High Mother and hold the crypt door so
nothing else gets up into the town. Before they go, she gives the heroes one
piece of advice: "Smash the skeletons. Swords just make them angry." If a
hero asks, she'll show them how to put their whole body into a swing (that
hero gets +1 to hit in Room 1). She'll even let a hero try lifting her great
mace. It's *very* heavy.

## Uncle Lucius Appears

As the heroes leave the temple, a kindly old man waves from the street (he
never comes up the temple steps: "Oh, my old knees, dear!"). He's heard
they're going below. He gives each hero a **Healing Potion** and a **Lucky
Coin** ("Once a day, give it a rub for luck!"). He also gives them a **map of the
upper catacombs**, which is accurate. Make him warm, funny and genuinely
likable.

## Room 1: The Broken Crypt (entrance and guardians)

> The crypt is cold and smells like old stone. Where the back wall used to be
> is a jagged hole, and beyond it, stairs made of *bones* spiral down into the
> dark. Click. Clack. Something is coming up the stairs.

- **4 Skeleton Warriors** climb out of the hole.
- Teach the fight rules here. Remind girls about **Healing Miracles** and
  **Holy Burst** (it destroys these skeletons instantly), and remind everyone
  that **smashing weapons** stop skeletons from putting themselves back together.
- **Treasure:** an old offering box: **2d6 gold**.

## Room 2: The Hall of Skulls (puzzle and conversation)

> The tunnel opens into a long hall. The walls are made of skulls, thousands
> of them, stacked like bricks. And one of them... winks at you.
> "Evening!" it says. "Want to hear a joke?"

- **Old Bartholomew** the talking skull. He'll tell bad jokes ("Why didn't
  the skeleton go to the party? He had no BODY to go with!"). He knows the way
  but wants payment: **a joke from each hero**. Every joke earns a ⭐ if it's
  funny or truly terrible.
- **The Three Tunnels:** three tunnels leave the hall, each with a carved
  symbol: a **skull**, a **crown**, and a **sunburst** (the sign of the Son).
  Bartholomew hints: "Follow the Son, never the bones."
  - **Sunburst** → the right way, to Room 3.
  - **Skull** → a dead end with **3 Shamblers**, then back.
  - **Crown** → a forgotten tomb: a **Glowing Helmet** (Helmet, +1 Armor,
    glows like a lantern) guarded by **2 Skeleton Warriors**. Optional
    treasure!
- Bartholomew asks to come along. If they take him, he gives hints (and bad
  jokes) for the rest of the quest.

## Room 3: The Flooded Ossuary (trap and setback)

> Water. Black, cold water, up to your knees, and bones bobbing on top like
> little boats. Halfway across the room you hear a *gurgle*, and something
> big and swollen rises out of the water.

- **1 Bloater** and **3 Shamblers** rise from the water. Warn them (with
  the Bloater's gross gurgling) to shoot it from far away. **Zombie Rot is
  possible here.**
- **The Trap:** the middle of the floor is rotten. The first hero to walk over
  it rolls **12+** or falls through to a lower tunnel (**d6** damage). The team
  can lower a rope, or jump down after them.
- **Treasure:** a drowned adventurer's pack: **3d6 gold** and a **Healing
  Potion**.

### ⏸ Good stopping point
> From somewhere far below, you hear a voice chanting... and a girl screaming
> "LEAVE ME ALONE!" Then a cat yowls. To be continued!

## Room 4: The Deep Chapel (the boss)

> The tunnel opens into a huge, ancient chapel deep underground. Faded
> paintings of the Son cover the walls. In the middle, a woman in tattered
> black robes raises her hands, green fire swirling around her fingers.
> On the floor, bones rattle and rise. "The little runaway is MINE," she
> hisses. "And so are you."

- **Sister Morda (Necromancer):** Armor 12 · Hearts 25 · Attack +5 · d8 dark
  bolt. She **raises 2 Shamblers every round** from the bones on the floor.
  **If Morda dies, all her undead collapse.**
- She starts with **4 Shamblers** around her. She stays back on the altar steps.
- **Pudding the cat** is hiding behind a pillar. A hero who goes to Pudding
  (roll 8+, or automatically for the 4-year-old) gets the cat to safety, and
  Pudding swipes at Morda (she has −2 to hit next round).
- Morda **can beg for mercy** at low hearts ("Please! He promised me power!
  The old man, he promised!"). The heroes decide what to do with her.
- **Treasure:** Morda's satchel: **4d6 gold**, a **Spark Wand**. On the old
  chapel altar, under dust: a **Holy Light Wand** (`rules/WEAPONS.md`).

## Room 5: Euonie's Hiding Place (reward and revelation)

> Behind the altar, a little door. Inside, a girl about thirteen years old
> holds up a shaking wand, her pointy hat crooked. "Stay back! I... oh. You're
> not skeletons."

- **Euonie** tells the truth (let the 13-year-old ask the questions):
  - The Xiphex coven tried a ritual for a good harvest. It was the
    wrong ritual. "It opened a door. A door to *Hell*. And something came
    through. Something with four arms. It's called **Shiva**."
  - "We got the ritual from a nice old man with a walking stick and a bag of
    sweets." (**Big clue!** Let the kids react. Don't confirm anything.)
  - Only one witch refused to do the ritual: the coven's Elder,
    **Grandmother Morwen**. "She fled to the **Floating Island**. If anyone
    knows how to close the portal, it's her."
  - How do you get to the Floating Island? "Catch one of its cats. Blue legs
    and belly, green back. I saw one in your town last week!"
- Euonie asks to stay at the temple. She can become a recurring friend.

## Conclusion

Back at the crypt door, Anamata is standing guard, surrounded by a pile of
smashed bones; a few skeletons got past the heroes, and none got past her.
She gives the heroes a rare smile and a nod. At the temple, Mother Ilsa pays **50 gold** (and the Holy Water if they
haven't had it), heals everyone, and Pudding gets a hero's dinner. The crypt
is sealed with a blessing. Hand out ⭐ generously.
