# Chapter 2: The Whisperwood

> The trees of the Whisperwood are so tall their tops disappear into the mist.
> And the leaves... are whispering. "Who's there? Who's there?" The trail of
> silver sparkle-dust drifts deeper into the woods.

## Scene 1: The Whispering Trees
The trees gossip and ask each hero a question ("What's your favorite food?
What are you scared of?"). If kids answer honestly, the trees part to show the
path. If they're rude, the branches tickle them until they giggle.

## Scene 2: The Mushroom Ring (choose a path)
The path splits:
- **Left**: a ring of giant glowing mushrooms. Stepping inside shrinks you to
  mouse size for a few minutes! Silly fun: they meet a bossy beetle general
  who needs help finding his lost helmet (an acorn cap). Reward: a **Beetle
  Whistle** that summons a beetle army once.
- **Right**: a patch of sticky honey-vines. Brave roll to pull free, or
  Clever to figure out that singing makes the vines relax.

## Scene 3: Grizzlewick's Trap
Grizzlewick has set a trap: a net hanging from a tree, baited with a jar of
"starlight" (really fireflies). If a hero grabs the jar, the net drops (no
heart lost, just stuck and embarrassed). Grizzlewick pops out cackling:
"Nobody's bringing back the light! When it's dark, everyone buys MY jars!"

He has **2 goblin helpers, Snip and Snorkel**, who are clumsy and kind of nice.
- Fight: Grizzlewick flees after 2 hits. The helpers surrender.
- Trick: he can't resist a bargain or a bet.
- Befriend: Snip and Snorkel can be convinced to switch sides! (If so, they
  become helpers who show up later with useful info.)

Let the kids free the fireflies. They float up and spell "THANK YOU" in the
sky. ⭐ for everyone.

## Ending
They camp under the stars. Kind roll or a cookie heals hearts. In the
distance, they hear the sad singing from the mountain for the first time.
