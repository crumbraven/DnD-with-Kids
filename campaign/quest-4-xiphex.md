# Quest 4: The Gates of Xiphex (FINALE)

*Location: the witches' town of Xiphex (say it "SHIFF-ecks"). Two sessions (or one long one).*

## Background (Game Master only)

The Empress's army attacks the undead horde outside Xiphex's walls. While
the horde is busy, the heroes use **Cinder** (the Xiphex cat) to slip
through the Veils, **carrying the Ark**, right into the town. They must cross
the burning town to the **Portal Square**, where **Shiva** guards the portal
and the **Devil** waits. Setting the Ark before the portal while a girl or
woman prays will **destroy the portal**. That ends the apocalypse and banishes
the Devil.

Most of the coven are still in town, hiding, sorry and scared.

## Cast

- **Cinder**: the grey cat with ember eyes. Brave for a cat.
- **Mother Hesper**: head of the surviving coven, hiding in the bakery.
  Ashamed and desperate to help.
- **Sister Vex**: a witch who has gone over to the Devil's side.
- **Shiva**: the four-armed demon guardian (`rules/MONSTERS.md`).
- **The Devil** (Uncle Lucius, revealed).

## Opening (read aloud)

> From the hilltop, you can see Xiphex. The sky above it is red, like the
> inside of a fire. A ring of undead surrounds the town, thousands of them,
> and below you, the Empress's army is lining up, banners snapping in the wind.
> Horns blow. The army charges. Now's your chance. Four of you lift the Ark by
> its golden poles. Someone holds Cinder. "Ready?" Cinder flicks her tail...
> and the world goes soft and shimmery.

Remind them: everyone must be **touching Cinder** or touching someone who is.
**Nobody touches the Ark.**

---

## Room 1: Ash Street (guardians)

> You land in a crooked street. Ash falls like grey snow. Pumpkin lanterns
> flicker in the windows of tilted witch houses. Shamblers in the street turn
> toward you, then stop and back away, moaning, from the Ark's golden glow.
> But two things don't back away. Two dogs made of fire.

- **2 Hellhounds** and **4 Imps**. (Undead can't approach the Ark, but demons can.)
- **Carrying the Ark in a fight:** bearers can't attack while carrying.
  Set it down (carefully, by the poles) to fight; it's safe on the ground,
  since demons won't touch it either.

## Room 2: The Bakery (conversation and a moral choice)

> A door creaks open. A woman in a flour-dusted witch's hat waves you in.
> "Quick! Inside!" The bakery is full of frightened witches, maybe a dozen of
> them, huddled behind the ovens.

- **Mother Hesper** begs forgiveness. "We were fools. An old man promised the
  ritual would bring a good harvest." This is a **choice for the kids**,
  especially the 13-year-old: **forgive them, or not?**
  - **Forgive:** the coven helps. They give the heroes **a Healing Potion
    each**, a secret path through the cellars (skip Room 3's ambush and go
    straight to the Square), and they'll fight beside the heroes at the
    portal (Shiva gets −1 attack per round while they hex her).
  - **Don't forgive:** they still give directions, but nothing else.
- **Sister Vex** is among them, secretly working for the Devil. She slips out
  the back to warn him (roll 14+ to notice her leaving). If caught, she
  confesses or fights (coven witch stats).

## Room 3: The Lying Bridge (setback)

*(Skip if the coven showed them the secret path.)*

> The bridge to the Portal Square crosses a river of black water. Halfway
> across, a voice calls from the fog. It sounds just like your mom: "Kids!
> Over here! I need help!"

- It's **the Devil's Lie**. Every hero must roll **16+** to see through it
  (+4 for anyone who's already suspicious, and the 13-year-old can warn the
  others for +2 each). Anyone fooled runs toward the voice and loses their turn.
- Then the trap springs: **1 Demon Brute** and **Sister Vex** (if she escaped)
  attack on the bridge.

### ⏸ Good stopping point
> Across the bridge, the street opens into a great square. In the middle,
> floating above a burned ritual circle, is a ring of swirling red-and-black
> fire, as tall as a house. The portal. And in front of it, something stands
> up. And up. And up. It has four arms.

---

## Room 4: The Portal Square (FINAL BATTLE)

> She's taller than the bakery. Four arms, each holding a sword made of fire.
> Eyes like burning coals. Behind her, shambling dead crawl out of the portal,
> one after another. "Bring me the box," says Shiva, "and I will let one of
> you live." Beside her, leaning on his pitchfork, the Devil smiles. "Hello
> again, my dears."

- **Shiva** (90 hearts, see `rules/MONSTERS.md`). The Ark weakens her: she
  loses 1 attack per round while it's in the Square.
- **The Devil** joins in. He **cannot be destroyed** while the portal is open,
  so encourage the kids to focus on Shiva or on getting the Ark to the
  portal.
- **Shamblers** crawl out of the portal (2 per round), but they can't get
  near the Ark.
- **Two ways to win:**
  1. **Defeat Shiva**, then carry the Ark to the portal.
  2. **Rush the portal:** four bearers carry the Ark across the Square (3
     rounds of moving while Shiva attacks), then a girl prays. Very brave!
- Give **every kid** a heroic moment in this fight. If a hero dies, a girl's
  Resurrection on the spot is a huge moment. Make it count.

## Room 5: Closing the Portal (the ending)

The Ark must be set down before the portal, and a **girl or woman must pray**.
*(If there are several girls, let them pray together. The 4-year-old can be
the one who says "Amen.")*

> The lid of the Ark doesn't open, but light pours out of it anyway: golden,
> warm, enormous, brighter than the sun. It flows toward the portal like a
> river. The red fire screams. The Devil shrieks, "NO! NO, NOT YET!", and his
> shadow is sucked back into the portal like water down a drain. With a sound
> like the biggest bell in the world... the portal shatters into a million
> sparks, and is gone.
>
> All across the realm, every skeleton falls into a pile of bones. Every
> zombie lies down and is still. In the catacombs under your home town,
> Old Bartholomew the skull starts singing.
>
> The sky above Xiphex turns blue.

## Epilogue

- The Empress holds a great celebration at the Castle Arc. Each hero is
  named a **Champion of the Realm** and gets **500 gold** and their choice of
  any treasure weapon or armor.
- Ask each kid: **"What does your hero do now?"**
- Loose threads for future adventures: Euonie becomes a witch of the new,
  better coven; the giant egg from the Floating Island hatches; the Devil's
  last words were "not yet"...

**THE END** (of this campaign!)
