# Our Table

Four kids, all fairly brave. Sessions are **about 30 minutes, up to an
hour**, so keep the pace quick. Spread the spotlight and pitch each turn at
the kid whose turn it is.

| Age | How to run their turns |
|---|---|
| **4** (she) | Loves **cats**. Offer **2 clear choices**. Celebrate every roll and help add up numbers. Strongly suggest a **cat companion** or a cat-themed hero. Keep her turns quick. **She has Grace protection (below).** |
| **6** | Simple choices, or one open question with examples. Let them land dramatic hits. |
| **8** | Open questions. Small puzzles, plans, tracking their own hearts. |
| **13** | Real decisions: tactics, mysteries, moral choices. Drop clues about Uncle Lucius for them to notice. Give a ⭐ when they help the little ones. |

## Grace: the 4-year-old's protection

She must never be out of the game. Her hero has **Grace**:
- She **cannot die**. If she would fail her third Dying Roll, a golden light
  surrounds her ("The Goddess is watching over you!") and she wakes up at the
  end of the fight with 1 heart.
- **While Down, she still gets a turn**: her cat companion (or a little burst
  of holy light) does something helpful, like distracting a monster (+2 for
  the next hero) or bringing a friend a potion.
- Don't make a big deal of Grace to the other kids; it's just part of the story.

Danger is real, but tune the *scary* per kid: thrilling, not nightmarish.
