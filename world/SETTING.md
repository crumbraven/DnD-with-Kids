## The Big Picture

A classic fantasy world of castles, knights, wizards, dragons and dark woods,
and it is **in the middle of a zombie and skeleton apocalypse**. The dead are
rising. Graveyards empty out at night, skeleton warbands march on the roads,
and whole villages have fallen. Survivors hide behind walls, and brave
adventurers are the land's best hope.

**Mood:** fun and fantastical, with **real danger**. Heroes can die. Monsters
die. Victories feel earned.

## Magic and Miracles

- **Magic is very common.** Wands, staffs, enchanted items and spellcasters
  are everywhere: the baker might light her oven with a spark spell.
- **Women and girls are naturally better at magic.**
- **Men and boys are naturally physically stronger.**
- **Only women and girls can perform Healing Miracles**, which are given to
  them by **God**. Healing is a gift from God, not a spell.
- See "Women & Men" and "Healing Miracles" in `rules/RULES.md` for how this
  works in the game.
