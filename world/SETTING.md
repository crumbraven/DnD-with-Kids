## The Big Picture

A classic fantasy world of castles, knights, wizards, dragons and dark woods,
and it is **in the middle of a zombie and skeleton apocalypse**. The dead are
rising. Graveyards empty out at night, skeletons march up out of the
catacombs, and whole villages have fallen. Survivors hide behind walls and
temple doors, and brave adventurers are the realm's best hope.

**Mood:** fun and fantastical, with **real danger**. Heroes can die (and be
brought back by miracles). Monsters die. Victories feel earned.

## What Caused It (Game Master secret!)

A **coven of witches** in the town of **Xiphex** *accidentally* opened a
**portal to Hell** during a ritual. Through it pours the power that raises the
dead, and through it came the demon **Shiva**, who now guards the portal with
a horde of undead.

*Even deeper secret:* the coven didn't find that ritual by accident. It was
given to them by a charming, kindly traveler named **Uncle Lucius**, who is
really **the Devil** in disguise (see "The Betrayer" below).

**The apocalypse can be ended** by reaching Xiphex and destroying the
portal.

## Faith: the Goddess, the Son, and the Church

- **God** and **the Goddess** are the same. God has no real gender, but
  most people say **the Goddess**, **the Lady**, or **my Lady** ("Thank you,
  my Lady!" "Lady preserve us!"). Use these in NPC speech.
- Her **Son** built the **Castle Arc** and founded the **Church of the Son**,
  then **ascended to Heaven**.
- The **Church of the Son** rules the realm. Its head is **the Empress**.
- **Temples** of the Church stand in most towns and every good-sized town.
  The Church is who **grants access to Healing Miracles**: girls and women are
  blessed at the temple. Temples are allies, sanctuaries, a place to be
  healed and resurrected, and where girls train in new miracles.

## The Empress

**Empress Mondenkind** (her name means **Moonchild**; common folk often
call her "the Moonchild"). A **good ruler** and the head of the Church of the
Son. She lives in the **Castle Arc**.

## Magic, Miracles and Holy Power

- **Magic is very common.** Wands, staffs, enchanted items and spellcasters
  are everywhere.
- **Women and girls are naturally better at magic. Men and boys are naturally
  physically stronger.**
- **Only women and girls can perform Healing Miracles**, granted through the
  Church.
- **All evil enemies, especially the undead, are very weak to holy power**
  (miracles, holy spells, holy water). A Healing Miracle **instantly destroys
  nearby undead** and **badly hurts demons**. It may hurt other evil things,
  but not always.
- Rules: see `rules/RULES.md` ("Healing Miracles", "Holy Damage").

## Cats and the Veils 🐈

- Reality has layers called **the Veils**. **Cats can slip between them** to
  travel great distances in a moment.
- Cats spend their days roaming far from home. **If you catch a cat, it slips
  through the Veils and takes you along to its homeland.**
- **A cat looks like the place it was born.** Sometimes a cat adopts a new
  homeland and slowly changes color.
- Known cat colors:
  - **Blue legs and belly, green back** → born on the **Floating Island**.
  - *(Claude may invent more: e.g. a sooty grey cat with ember-orange eyes
    from Xiphex, a gold-and-white cat from the Castle Arc, a mossy cat
    from the deep woods.)*
- Catching a cat is hard: they're quick, clever and slippery. It's a fun
  challenge with dice (see the quests).

## Places

- **The Catacombs**: run beneath almost all of civilization, **10 to 100
  meters deep**. Tunnels lined with bones and skulls, like real catacombs.
  Since the apocalypse they're overrun with skeletons and monsters.
- **The Castle Arc**: the Empress's home, built by the Son. Above ground and
  a few levels below, it's a normal (magnificent) castle. **Below that is a
  vast labyrinth.** At the heart of the labyrinth is **the Ark of the
  Covenant**: the same Ark held by the ancient Hebrews. It holds the holy
  power of God. **No one may touch it and live**; it is carried only on its
  poles.
- **The Floating Island**: drifts in the sky. Reached by catching one of its
  blue-and-green cats.
- **Xiphex** (pronounced *SHIFF-ecks*): the witches' town, where the portal to Hell is open. Now
  surrounded by undead.
- **The heroes' home base**: decided during character creation. It has a
  temple.

## The Betrayer: Uncle Lucius

A kindly old traveler with a warm laugh, a walking stick and a bottomless
bag of treats and helpful potions. He shows up when the heroes need help and
he *does* help, for a while. **He is the Devil.** He gave the witches the
ritual, and he wants the heroes to fail at the last moment. He lies, he
flatters, and he seems nice. See the quests for when he betrays them.

Hints to drop for the 13-year-old: holy water makes him sneeze; cats hiss at
him; he never sets foot in a temple ("my old knees, dear"); his shadow
sometimes doesn't match his body.
