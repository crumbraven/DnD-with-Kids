# Chapter 1: The Dark Village

## Opening (read this aloud)

> The village of Puddlebrook is dark. *Very* dark. For three nights the magic
> Glow Lanterns have gone out, and nobody knows why. Tonight, the whole village
> has gathered in the town square, holding candles. Mayor Wobblebottom climbs
> onto a barrel. "Citizens! We need HEROES!" He squints around. "Has anyone
> seen my spectacles?" (They're on his head.) Then his eyes land on... you.

Have each hero introduce themselves: "Tell everyone who you are and show off
your Special Power!" Give a ⭐ to each kid for a great introduction.

## Scene 1: The Mayor's Problem
The Mayor begs them to find out where the starlight went. He offers a
reward: a lifetime supply of Puddlebrook's famous honey buns.

## Scene 2: Investigate the Village (let kids choose where to go)
- **Ma Fennick's Lantern Shop**: she explains lanterns hold starlight from the
  Moonwell on Hush Mountain. She gives the team **one Glow Lantern (empty)**
  and cookies. Clue: "On the night it went dark, I heard *singing*. Sad singing,
  from the mountain."
- **The Big Lantern in the square**: a roll of 12+ notices the lantern
  glass is fine but a trail of **silver sparkle-dust** floats away from it
  toward the Whisperwood, like the light was *pulled* out.
- **The Duck Pond**: the ducks are upset. Roll 12+ to talk with them:
  they saw "a stream of light flying through the sky toward the mountain."
  Quack the duck decides to join the party, whether they want him or not.
- **A stranger's cart**: a goblin in a big hat is selling "Genuine Starlight,
  only 10 coins!" He packs up fast and scurries away if questioned. (This is
  Grizzlewick. Kids may chase him. He escapes in a puff of glitter and drops a
  **map with Hush Mountain circled**.)

## Scene 3: Little Trouble
Before leaving, a group of **3 Mischief Mice** in the dark granary is stealing
the village's grain and squeaking rude words. Easy challenge: they can be
chased, tricked or bribed with cheese. Good first practice at rolling dice.

## Ending
The trail of sparkle-dust leads to the edge of the **Whisperwood**. Ma Fennick
waves: "Bring back our light, little ones!" End on the trees' leaves
whispering their names...
