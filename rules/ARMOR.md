# Armor

Your **Armor number** is what a monster must roll (d20 + its Attack) to hit
you. Bigger is safer. Base Armor with no armor at all is **10**.

## Starter Armor

| Armor | Armor # | Special |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday Clothes | 10 | Super comfy. +2 when sneaking or swimming. |
| Wizard Robes | 11 | +1 to hit with Clever magic weapons. |
| Padded Jacket | 11 | Quiet and warm. |
| Leather Armor | 12 | The adventurer's classic. |
| Chain Shirt | 14 | Jingles! −2 when sneaking. |
| Knight's Plate | 16 | CLANK! −4 when sneaking, and you sink when swimming. |

## Extras (add to your Armor #)

| Item | Bonus | Special |
|---|---|---|
| Shield | +2 | Needs a free hand (no two-handed weapons). Can use Shield Bash. |
| Helmet | +1 | Bonk-proof: a natural 1 from a monster does zero damage. |

| Dragonscale Vest | 15 | Fire and ice attacks do half damage. |
| Starlight Armor | 17 | No sneaking penalty. Glows when monsters are near. |
| Lucky Charm (extra) | +1 | Once per day, make a monster re-roll a hit on you. |

## Quick Guide for New Heroes

- **Brave hero:** Sword + Shield + Chain Shirt → Armor **16**, d8 damage.
- **Brave smasher:** Battle Hammer + Leather → Armor **12**, d10 damage.
- **Clever archer:** Short Bow + Leather → Armor **12**, shoots from far away.
- **Clever wizard:** Spark Wand + Wizard Robes → Armor **11**, zaps from afar.
- **Kind helper:** Shepherd's Staff + Padded Jacket → Armor **11**, trips foes.
- **Kind defender:** Bubble Wand + Shield + Leather → Armor **14**.
