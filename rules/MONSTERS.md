# Monster Compendium

**Stat line:** **Armor** (what heroes need on d20 + bonuses to hit) ·
**Hearts** (damage it can take) · **Attack** (Game Master's d20 bonus vs a
hero's Armor) · **Damage** (die rolled when it hits).

Every monster has a way to **befriend** it and a way it **gives up**. Nothing
dies in this game. Defeated monsters run, surrender, sleep or poof away.

> Balance tip: a group of four starting heroes does about **8–10 damage a
> round** to Armor 12. A fight with "Small" monsters lasts 1–2 rounds; a Boss
> lasts 4–6.

## Make-Your-Own-Monster Table (Game Master quick reference)

| Size | Armor | Hearts | Attack | Damage | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | 8–11 | 3–6 | +1 to +2 | d4 | Swarms, 1 per hero |
| Small | 11–13 | 6–12 | +3 | d6 | Groups of 2–4 |
| Medium | 13–15 | 15–25 | +4 | d8 | 1–2 with helpers |
| Big | 13–16 | 30–45 | +5 | d10 | A big scene by itself |
| Boss | 15–17 | 45–70 | +6 | d12 | End of a chapter |

Tough armor → fewer hearts. Soft armor → more hearts.

## Tiny

### Mischief Mouse
**Armor 10 · Hearts 3 · Attack +1 · Damage d4** (nibble)
- Comes in swarms of 3–6. Squeaks rude words.
- **Befriend:** cheese (offer food and roll 8+).
- **Gives up:** scurries into a hole, squeaking "No fair!"

### Grumble Bee
**Armor 11 · Hearts 4 · Attack +2 · Damage d4** (bzzzt)
- Flies: Up-close weapons need reach (spear) or the bee must swoop close.
- **Befriend:** flowers or honey.
- **Gives up:** buzzes off grumbling.

### Puddle Slime
**Armor 8 · Hearts 8 · Attack +1 · Damage d4** (sticky slap)
- When it hits, you're **stuck** and can't move next turn.
- Hit by d10 or d12 damage: it **splits** into two Tiny slimes with 3 hearts each.
- **Befriend:** give it a bath (it just wants to be clean).
- **Gives up:** melts into a harmless puddle and burps.

### Snap Crab
**Armor 14 · Hearts 5 · Attack +2 · Damage d6** (pinch!)
- Hard shell, soft belly: a hero who flips it (roll 12+) gets +4 to hit it.
- **Befriend:** a shiny pebble.
- **Gives up:** scuttles sideways into the sea.

## Small

### Goblin Sneak
**Armor 12 · Hearts 7 · Attack +3 · Damage d6** (rusty dagger)
- Runs away at half hearts if its friends are losing.
- **Befriend:** a good trade (goblins LOVE trading). Roll 12+.
- **Gives up:** "We surrender! Please don't tell our boss!"

### Goblin Slinger
**Armor 11 · Hearts 6 · Attack +3 · Damage d4** (rock, from far away)
- Hides behind things: +2 Armor while behind cover.
- **Befriend / gives up:** same as the Goblin Sneak.

### Bandit Raccoon
**Armor 13 · Hearts 8 · Attack +3 · Damage d4** (scratch)
- When it hits, it **steals** one item and tries to run off with it.
- **Befriend:** trade it something shiny for the stolen thing.
- **Gives up:** drops everything it stole and waddles away.

### Bog Imp
**Armor 12 · Hearts 8 · Attack +3 · Damage d6** (mud ball, from far away)
- Once per fight: turns **invisible** for a round (heroes need a natural 15+ to hit).
- **Befriend:** laugh at its jokes. They're terrible.
- **Gives up:** sinks into the mud, sulking.

### Rattlebones (a silly skeleton)
**Armor 13 · Hearts 10 · Attack +3 · Damage d6** (bony bonk)
- At 0 hearts it falls apart, then **puts itself back together** at 5 hearts the
  next round. Doesn't work if the final hit came from a club, hammer or
  Shield Bash.
- **Befriend:** play it music; it can't stop dancing.
- **Gives up:** clatters into a pile and grumbles about its back.

### Grey Wolf
**Armor 12 · Hearts 11 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (bite)
- **Pack:** +2 to hit if another wolf is next to the same hero.
- **Befriend:** food and a calm voice (roll 12+). Becomes a loyal pet!
- **Gives up:** howls and runs into the trees.

## Medium

### Goblin Boss
**Armor 15 · Hearts 18 · Attack +4 · Damage d8** (big cleaver)
- Shouts orders: all other goblins get **+1 to hit** while the Boss is up.
- **Befriend:** respect. Offer it a fancy title. Roll 16+.
- **Gives up:** throws down the cleaver and stomps off, pouting.

### Fuzzy Cave Spider
**Armor 13 · Hearts 16 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (fangs)
- Once per fight: shoots a **web**. The hero is stuck until they roll 12+.
- *(Skip this one if any kid is scared of spiders. Use a giant beetle instead.)*
- **Befriend:** it's lonely in the dark. Give it a light.
- **Gives up:** scuttles to the ceiling and hides.

### Cackle Witch
**Armor 12 · Hearts 20 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (zap)
- Once per fight: turns a hero into a **frog** for 1 round (they can only hop
  and ribbit).
- **Befriend:** compliment her hat. Seriously. Roll 12+.
- **Gives up:** flies off on her broom yelling "I'll get you next time!"

### Fibber Light
**Armor 15 · Hearts 8 · Attack +4 · Damage d4** (zap)
- Floats and glows. Tries to lead heroes the wrong way.
- Roll 16+ to see through its tricks.
- **Befriend:** play a game of tag with it.
- **Gives up:** blinks out like a birthday candle.

### Mossy Golem
**Armor 16 · Hearts 22 · Attack +4 · Damage d10** (stone slam)
- Slow: attacks only **every other round**.
- Has a glowing rune (roll 16+ to spot it). Hitting the rune does double damage.
- **Befriend:** it was built to guard something. Show it that the danger is gone.
- **Gives up:** sits down and turns back into a mossy hill.

## Big

### Grumbear
**Armor 13 · Hearts 30 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (squeeze-hug)
- Attacks **twice** per round (two different heroes).
- **Befriend:** honey. It drops everything for honey.
- **Gives up:** waddles off to find a nap spot.

### Bridge Troll
**Armor 13 · Hearts 30 · Attack +5 · Damage d10** (log club)
- **Regrows 2 hearts** every round unless hit by fire or a Spark Wand.
- Once per fight: **Snore-Sneeze**. Every hero takes d4 and is knocked back.
- **Befriend:** it's lonely. Say hello. Tell it a joke. Roll 12+.
- **Gives up:** "OKAY OKAY! You can cross!"

### Grub the Ogre
**Armor 11 · Hearts 35 · Attack +5 · Damage d12** (giant club)
- Not very smart: **tricks succeed on 8+**.
- **Befriend:** cook it a meal.
- **Gives up:** sits down and cries big ogre tears.

### Gadget-Golem
**Armor 15 · Hearts 24 · Attack +4 · Damage d8** (pots-and-pans punch)
- Has a big switch on its back that says **DO NOT PRESS**. Get behind it
  (roll 12+) and press it: the golem shuts off.
- **Befriend:** it's a machine. Reprogram it (roll 16+).
- **Gives up:** falls apart in a clatter of kitchenware.

## Boss

### Ember Drakeling (young dragon)
**Armor 16 · Hearts 45 · Attack +6 · Damage d10** (bite)
- Once per fight: **Fire Puff**. Every hero takes **d12**, or half on a
  dodge roll of 12+.
- Flies: when it's in the air only far-away and magic weapons can reach it.
- **Befriend:** dragons love treasure and being told they're magnificent. Roll 16+.
- **Gives up:** flies to its mountain and won't bother anyone again.

### Bumblefoot the Hill Giant
**Armor 14 · Hearts 60 · Attack +6 · Damage d12** (stomp)
- Throws **boulders** at heroes far away (d10).
- Trick: get it to look the wrong way, and everyone gets a free attack.
- **Befriend:** play a giant-sized game with it.
- **Gives up:** sits down so hard the ground shakes, and asks for peace.

### Sea Serpent
**Armor 15 · Hearts 50 · Attack +6 · Damage d10** (tail sweep)
- **Tail Sweep:** once per fight, attacks every hero at once.
- Dives underwater: +4 Armor until it comes up.
- **Befriend:** rescue its babies, or sing to it.
- **Gives up:** sinks into the deep with a sad *bloop*.
