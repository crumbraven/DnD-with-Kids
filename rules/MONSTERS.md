# Monster Compendium

**Stat line:** **Armor** (what heroes need on d20 + bonuses to hit) ·
**Hearts** (damage it can take) · **Attack** (Game Master's d20 bonus vs a
hero's Armor) · **Damage** (die rolled when it hits).

**Monsters die at 0 hearts.** Many have a way to be **befriended** instead,
and an **If it flees** line: what it does when it's down to its last few
hearts and decides to run or beg for mercy. The heroes can chase it, finish
it off, or let it go.

> Balance tip: a group of four starting heroes does about **8–10 damage a
> round** to Armor 12. A fight with "Small" monsters lasts 1–2 rounds; a Boss
> lasts 4–6.

## Make-Your-Own-Monster Table (Game Master quick reference)

| Size | Armor | Hearts | Attack | Damage | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | 8–11 | 3–6 | +1 to +2 | d4 | Swarms, 1 per hero |
| Small | 11–13 | 6–12 | +3 | d6 | Groups of 2–4 |
| Medium | 13–15 | 15–25 | +4 | d8 | 1–2 with helpers |
| Big | 13–16 | 30–45 | +5 | d10 | A big scene by itself |
| Boss | 15–17 | 45–70 | +6 | d12 | End of a chapter |

Tough armor → fewer hearts. Soft armor → more hearts.

## ☠️ THE UNDEAD (the apocalypse)

### Undead Rules (apply to everything in this section)
- **Undead never flee and never beg.** They fight until destroyed.
- **Fire:** fire attacks (Fire Orb, Spark Wand, torches) do **+2 damage**.
- **Holy power destroys them.** A Healing Miracle's **Holy Burst** destroys
  ordinary undead nearby instantly. **Holy damage** (holy water, blessed
  weapons, holy spells) is **doubled** against them. See `RULES.md`.
- **Skeletons** are weak to smashing: clubs, hammers, staffs and Shield Bash do
  **+2 damage**. Arrows, sling stones and daggers do **half** (round up).
- **Zombie Rot:** when a zombie's bite hits, the hero also gets 1 **Rot**.
  Each Rot lowers your **max hearts by 1**. A Healing Miracle or Holy Water
  cleans out all Rot. If Rot ever gets your max hearts to 0, you turn... so
  get it cleaned!
- **The Head Shot:** a natural 20 against any zombie destroys it instantly.

### Shambler (zombie)
**Armor 8 · Hearts 12 · Attack +2 · Damage d6** (bite + Rot)
- Slow, always goes last. Rarely alone: usually 3–8 at a time.
- Moans when it smells the living, so the next ones are coming.

### Runner (fast zombie)
**Armor 11 · Hearts 8 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (bite + Rot)
- Fast: **goes first**, before the heroes.
- Often leads a pack of Shamblers.

### Bloater
**Armor 8 · Hearts 15 · Attack +2 · Damage d4** (slam)
- When it dies, it **BURSTS**: everyone next to it takes **d8**
  (roll 12+ to jump clear and take none). Smart heroes shoot it from far away.

### Zombie Horde
**Armor 8 · Hearts 40 · Attack +4 · Damage d8** (many hands)
- A wave of zombies that counts as one big monster.
- Attacks **every hero next to it** each round.
- Fire Orb and other attacks that hit "every monster in a group" do **double
  damage** to a Horde.
- At 20 hearts it starts to break up into a few Shamblers.

### Skeleton Warrior
**Armor 13 · Hearts 10 · Attack +3 · Damage d6** (rusty sword)
- At 0 hearts it collapses, then **pulls itself back together** at 5 hearts
  next round, *unless* the final blow was smashing, fire or holy.

### Skeleton Archer
**Armor 12 · Hearts 8 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (arrows, from far away)
- Stays at the back. +2 Armor behind cover.

### Ghoul
**Armor 13 · Hearts 18 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (claws)
- Hungry and clever. When it hits, the hero must roll **12+** or be
  **frozen** (can't act) on their next turn.

### Wraith (ghost)
**Armor 14 · Hearts 20 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (cold touch)
- Floats through walls. Ordinary weapons do **half damage**; magic, fire,
  holy water and blessed weapons do full damage.

### Bone Knight
**Armor 17 · Hearts 30 · Attack +5 · Damage d10** (black greatsword)
- Commands the dead: skeletons near it get **+1 to hit**.
- Once per fight: **Dread Shout**. Every hero rolls 12+ or loses their next turn.

### Necromancer (living villain)
**Armor 12 · Hearts 25 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (dark bolt, from far away)
- Each round raises **2 Shamblers** from any bodies or graves nearby.
- **If the Necromancer dies, every undead it raised collapses.**
- Unlike the undead, it **can** flee or beg for its life.

### Bone Dragon (boss)
**Armor 16 · Hearts 70 · Attack +7 · Damage d12** (bite)
- Once per fight: **Grave Breath**. Every hero takes **d10** and 1 Rot
  (roll 12+ for half damage, no Rot).
- Flies: in the air, only far-away and magic weapons reach it.
- Smashing weapons do +2 (it's a skeleton).

## 🔥 DEMONS (from the portal to Hell)

### Demon Rules
- **Weak to holy:** holy damage is doubled, and a Healing Miracle's Holy Burst
  deals **3d6** to every demon close by.
- **Fire does nothing** to demons. They came from Hell.
- Demons can **lie, bargain and tempt**. Never trust a deal with a demon.

### Imp
**Armor 13 · Hearts 6 · Attack +3 · Damage d4** (tiny pitchfork)
- Flies, giggles, and steals small items. Comes in groups of 3–5.
- **If it flees:** pops back into Hell with a puff of sulfur smoke.

### Hellhound
**Armor 13 · Hearts 16 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (burning bite)
- Fast: **goes first**. Hunts in pairs.
- Once per fight: **Fire Breath** on one hero (d10, roll 12+ for half).

### Demon Brute
**Armor 15 · Hearts 30 · Attack +5 · Damage d10** (claws)
- Big, horned and red. Attacks **twice** per round.
- Hates holy water: if hit by it, it loses its next turn howling.

### Shiva, Guardian of the Portal (FINAL BOSS)
**Armor 17 · Hearts 90 · Attack +7 · Damage d12** (four burning blades)
- A towering demon with **four arms**, each holding a blade of hellfire.
- **Four Arms:** each round Shiva attacks **up to 4 times**, at different
  heroes or the same one.
- **Hellfire Ring** (at 45 hearts, once): fire bursts out. Every hero takes
  **2d6** (roll 14+ for half).
- **Call the Horde:** each round while the portal is open, **2 Shamblers**
  crawl out of it.
- **Weak to holy:** doubled holy damage, 3d6 from every Holy Burst. The Ark's
  presence makes her **lose 1 attack per round**.
- **Never flees.** If Shiva dies, she's dragged back into Hell screaming.

### Uncle Lucius / The Devil
**Disguised:** a kind old traveler. He doesn't fight; he "helps."
**Revealed:** **Armor 18 · Hearts 66 · Attack +7 · Damage d10** (shadow-claws)
- **Cannot be destroyed while the portal is open.** At 0 hearts he melts
  into shadow, laughing, and reappears later.
- **Lies:** once per fight he can make one hero believe something false
  (roll 16+ to see through it). He might pretend a friend is a monster, or
  say "your sister is in danger over there!"
- **Bargains:** offers the heroes something they *really* want in exchange
  for one small thing. It's always a trap.
- **Weak to holy:** holy water makes him sneeze and smoke; Holy Burst deals
  3d6.
- **Banished forever** when the portal is destroyed.

### The Xiphex Coven (witches, living)
**Armor 12 · Hearts 14 · Attack +4 · Damage d8** (hex bolt)
- Most of the coven are **sorry and scared**. They didn't mean to do it.
  Some might help the heroes; one or two have gone bad, tempted by Lucius.
- Once per fight: **Hex**. A hero has −2 on all rolls until the end of their
  next turn.

## The Living Monsters (standard fantasy fare)

### Tiny

#### Mischief Mouse
**Armor 10 · Hearts 3 · Attack +1 · Damage d4** (nibble)
- Comes in swarms of 3–6. Squeaks rude words.
- **Befriend:** cheese (offer food and roll 8+).
- **If it flees:** scurries into a hole, squeaking "No fair!"

#### Grumble Bee
**Armor 11 · Hearts 4 · Attack +2 · Damage d4** (bzzzt)
- Flies: Up-close weapons need reach (spear) or the bee must swoop close.
- **Befriend:** flowers or honey.
- **If it flees:** buzzes off grumbling.

#### Puddle Slime
**Armor 8 · Hearts 8 · Attack +1 · Damage d4** (sticky slap)
- When it hits, you're **stuck** and can't move next turn.
- Hit by d10 or d12 damage: it **splits** into two Tiny slimes with 3 hearts each.
- **Befriend:** give it a bath (it just wants to be clean).
- **If it flees:** melts into a harmless puddle and burps.

#### Snap Crab
**Armor 14 · Hearts 5 · Attack +2 · Damage d6** (pinch!)
- Hard shell, soft belly: a hero who flips it (roll 12+) gets +4 to hit it.
- **Befriend:** a shiny pebble.
- **If it flees:** scuttles sideways into the sea.

### Small

#### Goblin Sneak
**Armor 12 · Hearts 7 · Attack +3 · Damage d6** (rusty dagger)
- Runs away at half hearts if its friends are losing.
- **Befriend:** a good trade (goblins LOVE trading). Roll 12+.
- **If it flees:** "We surrender! Please don't tell our boss!"

#### Goblin Slinger
**Armor 11 · Hearts 6 · Attack +3 · Damage d4** (rock, from far away)
- Hides behind things: +2 Armor while behind cover.
- **Befriend / If it flees:** same as the Goblin Sneak.

#### Bandit Raccoon
**Armor 13 · Hearts 8 · Attack +3 · Damage d4** (scratch)
- When it hits, it **steals** one item and tries to run off with it.
- **Befriend:** trade it something shiny for the stolen thing.
- **If it flees:** drops everything it stole and waddles away.

#### Bog Imp
**Armor 12 · Hearts 8 · Attack +3 · Damage d6** (mud ball, from far away)
- Once per fight: turns **invisible** for a round (heroes need a natural 15+ to hit).
- **Befriend:** laugh at its jokes. They're terrible.
- **If it flees:** sinks into the mud, sulking.

#### Grey Wolf
**Armor 12 · Hearts 11 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (bite)
- **Pack:** +2 to hit if another wolf is next to the same hero.
- **Befriend:** food and a calm voice (roll 12+). Becomes a loyal pet!
- **If it flees:** howls and runs into the trees.

### Medium

#### Goblin Boss
**Armor 15 · Hearts 18 · Attack +4 · Damage d8** (big cleaver)
- Shouts orders: all other goblins get **+1 to hit** while the Boss is up.
- **Befriend:** respect. Offer it a fancy title. Roll 16+.
- **If it flees:** throws down the cleaver and stomps off, pouting.

#### Fuzzy Cave Spider
**Armor 13 · Hearts 16 · Attack +4 · Damage d6** (fangs)
- Once per fight: shoots a **web**. The hero is stuck until they roll 12+.
- *(Skip this one if any kid is scared of spiders. Use a giant beetle instead.)*
- **Befriend:** it's lonely in the dark. Give it a light.
- **If it flees:** scuttles to the ceiling and hides.

#### Cackle Witch
**Armor 12 · Hearts 20 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (zap)
- Once per fight: turns a hero into a **frog** for 1 round (they can only hop
  and ribbit).
- **Befriend:** compliment her hat. Seriously. Roll 12+.
- **If it flees:** flies off on her broom yelling "I'll get you next time!"

#### Fibber Light
**Armor 15 · Hearts 8 · Attack +4 · Damage d4** (zap)
- Floats and glows. Tries to lead heroes the wrong way.
- Roll 16+ to see through its tricks.
- **Befriend:** play a game of tag with it.
- **If it flees:** blinks out like a birthday candle.

#### Mossy Golem
**Armor 16 · Hearts 22 · Attack +4 · Damage d10** (stone slam)
- Slow: attacks only **every other round**.
- Has a glowing rune (roll 16+ to spot it). Hitting the rune does double damage.
- **Befriend:** it was built to guard something. Show it that the danger is gone.
- **If it flees:** sits down and turns back into a mossy hill.

### Big

#### Grumbear
**Armor 13 · Hearts 30 · Attack +5 · Damage d8** (squeeze-hug)
- Attacks **twice** per round (two different heroes).
- **Befriend:** honey. It drops everything for honey.
- **If it flees:** waddles off to find a nap spot.

#### Bridge Troll
**Armor 13 · Hearts 30 · Attack +5 · Damage d10** (log club)
- **Regrows 2 hearts** every round unless hit by fire or a Spark Wand.
- Once per fight: **Snore-Sneeze**. Every hero takes d4 and is knocked back.
- **Befriend:** it's lonely. Say hello. Tell it a joke. Roll 12+.
- **If it flees:** "OKAY OKAY! You can cross!"

#### Grub the Ogre
**Armor 11 · Hearts 35 · Attack +5 · Damage d12** (giant club)
- Not very smart: **tricks succeed on 8+**.
- **Befriend:** cook it a meal.
- **If it flees:** sits down and cries big ogre tears.

#### Gadget-Golem
**Armor 15 · Hearts 24 · Attack +4 · Damage d8** (pots-and-pans punch)
- Has a big switch on its back that says **DO NOT PRESS**. Get behind it
  (roll 12+) and press it: the golem shuts off.
- **Befriend:** it's a machine. Reprogram it (roll 16+).
- **If it flees:** falls apart in a clatter of kitchenware.

### Boss

#### Ember Drakeling (young dragon)
**Armor 16 · Hearts 45 · Attack +6 · Damage d10** (bite)
- Once per fight: **Fire Puff**. Every hero takes **d12**, or half on a
  dodge roll of 12+.
- Flies: when it's in the air only far-away and magic weapons can reach it.
- **Befriend:** dragons love treasure and being told they're magnificent. Roll 16+.
- **If it flees:** flies to its mountain and won't bother anyone again.

#### Bumblefoot the Hill Giant
**Armor 14 · Hearts 60 · Attack +6 · Damage d12** (stomp)
- Throws **boulders** at heroes far away (d10).
- Trick: get it to look the wrong way, and everyone gets a free attack.
- **Befriend:** play a giant-sized game with it.
- **If it flees:** sits down so hard the ground shakes, and asks for peace.

#### Sea Serpent
**Armor 15 · Hearts 50 · Attack +6 · Damage d10** (tail sweep)
- **Tail Sweep:** once per fight, attacks every hero at once.
- Dives underwater: +4 Armor until it comes up.
- **Befriend:** rescue its babies, or sing to it.
- **If it flees:** sinks into the deep with a sad *bloop*.
