# Dice & Daring: The Whole Rulebook

*You need a regular dice set: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20.*
*The **d20** decides IF something works. The other dice decide HOW MUCH.*

Also see: `WEAPONS.md`, `ARMOR.md`, `MONSTERS.md`, `SHOP.md`.

## Your Hero

- **A name and what they are.** Anything goes: a knight, a wizard frog, a robot
  who loves flowers, a dragon kid who can't breathe fire yet.
- **Hero Bonus: +2.** Add it to **every d20 roll** you make. It goes up when
  you level up.
- **One Special Power** they invent. Use it **once per fight or scene** and it
  just works, no roll needed.
- **12 Hearts** ❤️ (that's your health; 14 for boys and men).
- **One weapon and one armor** from the Starter lists (a shield too, if you
  have a free hand).
- **Armor number** = your armor's number (+2 with a shield).

## Women & Men

In this world:
- **Girls and women** are better at magic: **+2 to hit** with magic weapons
  (wands, staffs, orbs). Only they can perform **Healing Miracles** (below).
- **Boys and men** are physically stronger: **+1 damage** with up-close
  weapons, and **+2 max hearts** (they start with 14).

## Healing Miracles ✨

Healing is a gift from God, granted to girls and women through the Church of
the Son.

- A girl or woman hero has **3 Miracles** a day. They come back after a night's
  sleep. Each level up adds **+1 Miracle**.
- **Heal:** on her turn she prays and touches someone (or herself). They get
  back **2d4 hearts**, and all their **Zombie Rot** is cleaned out. A hero who
  is **Down** gets right back up.
- **Holy Burst (every heal does this):** the holy light blazes out.
  - Every **ordinary undead** close by (a few steps) is **destroyed
    instantly**.
  - **Big and Boss undead** (Bone Knight, Bone Dragon and the like) take **3d6**
    instead.
  - **Demons** close by take **3d6**.
  - Other evil creatures *might* be hurt (Game Master decides: usually d6).
- **Resurrection:** spend **2 Miracles at once** on a hero who has died (as
  long as it's the same day). They come back to life with **half their
  hearts**.
- Girls can learn more miracles at temples (see `SHOP.md`).

## Holy Damage 🌟

Holy water, blessed weapons and holy spells deal **holy damage**. Holy damage
is **doubled** against undead and demons: roll it, then double it!

## Trying Something Tricky (not fighting)

Roll the **d20** and add your **Hero Bonus**. Match or beat the target:

| How hard? | Need |
|---|---|
| Easy | 8 |
| Normal | 12 |
| Hard | 16 |
| Super hard | 20 |

- **Natural 1** (the die shows 1): Oops! It goes wrong in a surprising way.
- **Natural 20**: AMAZING! It works in the coolest way possible.

## Fighting

1. **Heroes go first**, around the table. Then the monsters go.
2. On your turn you can **move and do one thing**: attack, cast, help a
   friend, drink a potion, or *talk* to the monster.
3. **To hit:** roll the **d20** + your weapon's Hit Bonus + your Hero
   Bonus. If you **match or beat the monster's
   Armor**, you hit!
4. **Damage:** roll your weapon's damage die (d4, d6, d8, d10 or d12). The
   monster loses that many hearts.
5. **Natural 20 = Critical Hit!** Roll your damage die twice and add them.
   **Natural 1** always misses (and something goofy happens).
6. Monsters attack the same way: the Game Master rolls a d20 + the monster's
   Attack against **your Armor**, then rolls the monster's damage die.

**When a monster hits 0 hearts, it dies.** Some monsters try to flee or beg
for mercy when they're nearly beaten, and the heroes decide what happens to
them. Some can be **befriended or tricked** instead of fought (see `MONSTERS.md`).

## Teamwork

Instead of attacking, you can **Help** a friend: describe how, and they
get **+2** on their next roll. Teamwork is the most powerful thing in the game.

## Hearts

- At **0 hearts** you're **Down**: you fall and can't do anything (see
  *Dying* below).
- **A short rest** (a snack break in the story) brings back **d6 hearts**.
  **A night's sleep** brings back all of them.
- **Healing Potion**: brings back **2d4 hearts**.

## Dying ☠️

When you're Down, at the start of each of your turns roll a **d20** (no
bonus). This is a **Dying Roll**:

- **10 or higher:** ✔️ a success. **Under 10:** ✖️ a fail.
- **3 successes:** you wake up with **1 heart**.
- **3 fails:** your hero **dies**.
- **Natural 20:** you leap back up with **3 hearts**!
- **Natural 1:** counts as **2 fails**.
- Getting hit while you're Down counts as **1 fail**.

**Saving a friend:** any healing (a potion, a healing power) gets them right
back up with those hearts. Or spend your turn next to them and roll **12+**
to stop the bleeding: they stop making Dying Rolls and wake up after the fight
with 1 heart.

**If a hero dies,** they can be brought back:
- by a girl or woman hero using **Resurrection** (2 Miracles), or
- at **any temple**, where a priestess will resurrect them for a donation
  of **20 gold** (free if the team is broke; the Church is kind).

Or the player can make a new hero who joins at the next scene.

*The youngest hero at our table has **Grace** and can't die (see
`world/TABLE.md`).*

## Gold 💰

Monsters, chests and grateful villagers give **gold coins**. Spend gold at
shops and temples on weapons, armor, potions, holy water and training (see
`SHOP.md`). The team can share or keep their own gold; each hero tracks theirs.

## Stars ⭐ and Leveling Up

The Game Master hands out ⭐ for great ideas, teamwork, being funny or helping a
sibling. **Spend a ⭐ to re-roll any die.**

When the whole team collects **10 ⭐**, everyone **Levels Up**:
- **+4 Hearts** (max)
- **+1 Hero Bonus** (so +3, then +4...)
- A **second Special Power** (at level 2), then a cooler weapon or armor, and so on.

## The Golden Rule

If the rules ever get in the way of fun, ignore the rules.
