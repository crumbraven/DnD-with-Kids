## Training (costs gold *and* needs a level up to learn)

At each level up, a hero can learn **one** of these (if they can find a teacher
and pay):

**Temple training, for girls and women (from the Church's priestesses), 30 gold each:**
- **Shield of Faith** (uses 1 Miracle): one hero gets **+3 Armor** for the rest of the fight.
- **Holy Smite** (uses 1 Miracle): your next hit does **+2d6 holy damage**.
- **Sanctuary** (uses 1 Miracle): for one round, no undead or demon can attack anyone close to you.
- **Bonus Miracle**: +1 Miracle per day.

**Knight training, for boys and men (from the Empress's knights), 30 gold each:**
- **Mighty Blow**: once per fight, roll your damage die **three times** and add them up.
- **Shield Wall**: once per fight, every hero next to you gets **+2 Armor** for a round.
- **Cleave**: when you defeat a monster, make a free attack on another one next to you.
- **Toughness**: +4 max hearts.

**Wizard training, for anyone (from town mages), 30 gold each:**
- **Light**: make anything glow like a lantern, as often as you like.
- **Magic Missile**: once per fight, it never misses and deals 2d4.
- **Feather Fall**: no damage from falling, for the whole team.

## Treasure Guide (Game Master)

Kids love loot, so be generous. Typical rewards:

| Found where | Gold |
|---|---|
| A Small monster's pockets | d6 |
| A Medium monster | 2d6 |
| A Big monster or a locked chest | 4d6 |
| A Boss or a hidden treasure room | 10d6 + a treasure weapon or armor |
| A grateful town, temple or the Empress | 50–100 |

Also hide **fun stuff**: a hat that makes you sound like a duck, boots that
squeak, a talking skull who tells bad jokes, a map, a key, a rare cat.
